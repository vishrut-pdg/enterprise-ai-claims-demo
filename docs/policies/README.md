# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy ID:** `expense-policy`\
**Policy Owner:** Finance\
**Applies To:** All employees submitting business expense claims\
**Effective Version:** v1

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for reimbursement of employee business expenses.

Employees are expected to submit accurate, complete, and timely claims with appropriate supporting documentation. Claims that do not meet policy requirements may be rejected or referred for further review.

The company may use automated controls and AI-assisted review to support expense assessment. Final processing remains subject to the rules and approval requirements defined in this policy.

---

## 2. Eligible Expenses

The company reimburses reasonable and necessary business expenses in the following categories:

- Meals
- Travel
- Supplies

Examples of eligible expenses include:

- client lunches and team meals;
- taxis, rail tickets, and hotel accommodation;
- office stationery and similar business supplies.

Expenses that are personal in nature or are unrelated to company business are not reimbursable.

Personal entertainment is not an eligible expense category.

---

## 3. Currency and Claim Amount

Expense claims must be submitted in **USD**.

Each claim must:

- have a total greater than USD 0.00;
- accurately reflect the sum of all expense lines;
- not exceed **USD 500.00** in total.

Claims above USD 500.00 do not meet the standard reimbursement limit and may be rejected in accordance with this policy.

Example:

A hotel expense claim of USD 680.00 exceeds the maximum permitted claim amount.

---

## 4. Receipts and Supporting Documentation

A valid receipt is required for every expense line.

The receipt must:

- relate to the expense being claimed;
- be attached to the appropriate expense line;
- be sufficiently clear to support verification.

Claims with missing or unverified receipts are not eligible for automatic approval and must be referred for further review.

Where additional information is required, the reviewer may request further documentation from the employee.

---

## 5. Duplicate Receipts

Receipts may be checked for duplicate use across expense claims.

Where a duplicate receipt is identified:

- the claim must be reviewed;
- the employee may be asked to provide clarification;
- the duplicate must not, by itself, be treated as evidence of fraud.

Any suspected misuse must be assessed based on the full claim context and available evidence.

---

## 6. Expense and Submission Dates

An expense must be incurred on or before the date the claim is submitted.

Expense claims must be submitted within **90 calendar days** of the expense date.

Claims submitted outside this period do not meet the standard submission requirement and may be subject to rejection or further review.

---

## 7. Claim Assessment

Expense claims are assessed against the requirements of this policy.

The assessment may include:

- eligibility of the expense category;
- claim currency;
- claim amount;
- receipt availability and validity;
- duplicate receipt checks;
- expense date;
- submission timeliness;
- completeness of supporting evidence.

Automated checks may be used to evaluate these requirements.

AI-assisted assessment may be used to interpret the claim, explain findings, and recommend an appropriate next step.

AI-assisted assessment does not replace the underlying policy rules.

---

## 8. Automatic Approval

A claim may be approved automatically only when all applicable policy requirements are satisfied.

Automatic approval requires:

- all mandatory policy checks to pass;
- all required supporting evidence to be available and verified;
- no unresolved questions;
- an AI recommendation to approve the claim;
- AI confidence of at least **90%**;
- automatic approval to be enabled for the applicable policy;
- confirmation that the claim has not changed since assessment.

Claims that do not meet all automatic approval requirements must be routed for further review.

---

## 9. Automatic Rejection

A claim may be rejected automatically only where there is a clear and explicit policy violation.

Automatic rejection requires:

- an explicit rejection condition to be identified;
- the rejection condition to be supported by the current claim data;
- the AI recommendation to be consistent with the policy finding;
- automatic rejection to be enabled for the applicable policy;
- confirmation that the claim has not changed since assessment.

Missing evidence, uncertainty, duplicate receipts, or low confidence do not automatically justify rejection unless another explicit policy violation exists.

---

## 10. Claims Requiring Review

A claim must be referred for manager review where the system cannot safely approve or reject it automatically.

Examples include:

- missing or unverified receipts;
- duplicate receipt findings;
- incomplete supporting information;
- unresolved questions;
- low-confidence assessments;
- conflicting findings;
- situations requiring management judgment.

A claim referred for review will have the status:

**Pending Manager Review**

Referral for review does not constitute approval or rejection.

---

## 11. Manager Review

Managers reviewing an expense claim may:

- Accept the claim;
- Reject the claim;
- Request more information.

Managers must provide a rationale for their decision.

The review record should include:

- claim details;
- applicable policy findings;
- supporting evidence;
- AI recommendation;
- unresolved questions;
- final manager decision;
- manager rationale;
- decision date and time.

---

## 12. Previous Decisions

Previous reviewed claims may be used as reference information when considering similar cases.

Historical decisions are intended to provide context only.

They must not:

- override the current policy;
- replace current supporting evidence;
- bypass required policy checks;
- automatically determine the outcome of a new claim.

The policy and evidence applicable to the current claim always take precedence.

---

## 13. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- submitting accurate expense information;
- selecting the correct expense category;
- providing valid supporting documentation;
- submitting claims within the required time period;
- responding to requests for additional information.

Submitting incomplete or inaccurate information may delay reimbursement.

---

## 14. Review and Audit

Expense claims and related decisions may be retained for audit, compliance, and process improvement purposes.

The company may record:

- policy checks;
- automated assessments;
- supporting evidence;
- approval and rejection decisions;
- manager reviews;
- system-generated recommendations;
- relevant processing history.

Automated recommendations do not remove the requirement for appropriate financial controls.

---

## 15. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must be explicitly approved by an authorized manager or Finance representative.

System-generated recommendations must not create or approve a policy exception unless a separate authorized exception process exists.
